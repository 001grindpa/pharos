How Pharos Works

01
Buy Cover
Prepay the monthly premium, specify the provider, covered calendar dates, incident date, and two independent status page sources.
02
Adjudicate
On or after the resolve date, validators independently check both public status pages to confirm whether the service outage occurred.
03
Settlement
Confirmed outages release credit to the customer. Otherwise, the provider receives the premium. Unresolved covers allow timeout return.
